Cello samples: "Cello Section", sustain with vibrato (susvib, v1), from the
Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition (VSCO-2-CE),
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).

Trimmed to 3 s, mixed to mono, loudness-matched and encoded as AAC.
Files are named by their true MIDI note (VSCO's names are one octave low):
36 = C2, 40 = E2, 43 = G2, 47 = B2, 50 = D3, 53 = F3, 57 = A3, 60 = C4, 64 = E4, 67 = G4.
